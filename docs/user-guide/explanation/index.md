# Explanation

Background and rationale for how Gloopy works — the *why*, not the steps. Read
these when you want to understand the design, not accomplish a specific task.

- **[Arrangement vs. Session](arrangement-vs-session.md)** — two ways to organise
  the same clips, when each earns its place, and why renders come only from the
  arrangement.
- **[The musical model](the-musical-model.md)** — beats, the tempo map, time
  signature, scales, and why everything is quarter-note-relative.
- **[Gain staging](gain-staging.md)** — why faders read 0–1 (normalized, not
  decibels), and how to keep a mix clean.
- **[How playback is scheduled](how-playback-is-scheduled.md)** — clips, loops, the
  playhead, and why the render is deterministic.

For the precise definitions these essays build on, see
[The Gloopy model](../../control-scripting/concepts/model.md).
