# Zoom and navigate the timeline

**Task:** get closer to the arrangement to edit detail, then travel a long song
without everything shrinking to unreadable slivers. Gloopy's timeline zooms on
three independent axes — time, track height, and waveform amplitude — and the zoom
and scroll position are saved with the project.

## Zoom time (horizontal)

- **Ctrl+scroll** (or a trackpad **pinch**) zooms around the pointer — the beat under
  the cursor stays put.
- **=** / **-** zoom in / out around the centre.
- The **`- + Fit`** cluster in the toolbar does the same for the mouse.
- **W** fits the whole song to the width (the old default).

## Travel the timeline

Once you're zoomed in past the width, move along the song with:

- the **horizontal scrollbar** pinned under the arrangement, or
- **Shift+scroll**, or
- **Ctrl+drag a box** (marquee) to jump straight to a region — it frames the box on
  both axes at once.

## Zoom track height (vertical)

- **Alt+scroll** makes the track rows taller or shorter.
- **H** fits all tracks to the available height.

Rows growing past the viewport scroll vertically as usual.

## Zoom waveform amplitude

For audio clips whose waveform is too flat to see:

- **Ctrl+Alt+scroll**, or **[** / **]**, scales the waveform overview — independently of
  track height, clamped to the lane.

## Zoom presets

Set up a couple of favourite views and flip between them:

- **Shift+1**…**Shift+5** stores the current view (all axes) in a slot.
- **1**…**5** recalls it.

## Frame the selection

- **F** zooms to the selected clip.
- **E** is a **zoom toggle** — jump to the selection, press **E** again to return exactly
  where you were.

## In the piano roll

The piano roll speaks the same language: **Ctrl+scroll** zooms time, **Alt+scroll** zooms
**pitch**, **Shift+scroll** scrolls, and **W** / **F** / **E** fit / frame / toggle. It has
its own **horizontal** (timeline) and **vertical** (pitch) scrollbars.
