# Browse content and pin favorites

Gloopy has a **browser** — a collapsible panel down the left side — that gathers the
things you drop into a project in one place. Toggle it with the **☰** button in the
toolbar. Along the top of the panel are tabs, and each row inside a tab does something
when you click it.

You can also **drag a row out of the browser and drop it onto the arrangement** — it
does the same thing as clicking (adds the plugin, imports the sample, seeds the
template, loads the patch), whichever you find quicker.

## The tabs

| Tab | Rows are… | Clicking a row |
|-----|-----------|----------------|
| **Templates** | starter projects (built-in and your own saved ones) | seeds a fresh project |
| **Demos** | bundled example songs | opens that example |
| **Plugins** | your installed instrument plugins | adds it as a new instrument track |
| **Samples** | audio files in your samples folder | imports it as an audio track |
| **Presets** | factory synth patches | adds a synth track loaded with that patch |
| **Favs** | your favorites (see below) | runs whatever the favorite points at |

## Favorites — pin the things you reach for

The **Favs** tab is your own shortlist. Instead of hunting through the plugin list or the
samples folder every time, pin the handful you actually use and they all sit together in
one tab.

- **To pin an item:** right-click any row in the Templates, Plugins, Samples, or Presets
  tab and choose **Add to Favorites**.
- **To use a favorite:** open the **Favs** tab and click it — it does exactly what the
  original row did (add the plugin, import the sample, seed the template, load the patch).
- **To unpin:** right-click the favorite in the **Favs** tab and choose **Remove from
  Favorites**.

Favorites are part of *your setup*, not the song — they follow you across every project,
the same way your saved templates and presets do. (They're kept in a small file in
Gloopy's application-data folder, so they persist between sessions.)
